At the core of Norway’s healthcare sector, the Norwegian Health Network is the hub everything interconnects through, spanning from the physical network wiring through datacenters to services. Having gone from Windows VMs to Kubernetes being the primary runtime environment, utilizing a model of one cluster per application at each site they need presence (no need to panic, as our Kubernetes lead phrased it: "It is only the largest open-source project after Linux, relax, it's easy to run yourself"), there has been a sudden and colossal change in observability-needs. This session will explore the observability stack we've built up around OpenTelemetry and Grafana to make teams and stakeholders capable of self-sufficiently navigating the new landscape, and highlight some key lessons learned during the adoption.
